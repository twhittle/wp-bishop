Directions

We are in Bel Air’s historic district — with one turn that catches first-time visitors.

Finding the office

The entrance is not on Rock Spring Road

Important: although our address is 612 Rock Spring Road, you cannot enter from Rock Spring Road. Turn onto E Howard Street and follow the alley approximately 200 feet from Rock Spring Road. We are the second house on the right.

Look for the 1886 Victorian — the Rebecca House. It is the building itself, not a suite inside a larger complex, so once you are on the alley it is hard to miss.
