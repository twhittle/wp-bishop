Estates & Trusts

Many clients find themselves in the position of managing the estate of a deceased family member, or administering a trust.

It is rarely a role anyone volunteers for, and it usually arrives at a difficult time. We work directly with the personal representative or trustee to facilitate the preparation of estate and trust tax returns and to make the requirements clear.

Our aim is that you understand what has to be filed, by when, and what your responsibilities are — so the administrative side takes as little of your attention as possible.

How we help

  • Preparation of estate and trust tax returns
  • Working alongside the personal representative or trustee
  • Clarifying filing requirements and deadlines
  • Coordinating with attorneys and other advisors
  • Ongoing trust compliance year to year
